About Us

Hi, I'm Craig Kohman, the owner of Southern Pro-Vision Landscape & Construction. I started this company with a simple goal: to help homeowners create outdoor spaces that look beautiful, function properly, and feel built around the way they actually live.

Whether we're designing a new landscape, improving irrigation, building hardscape features, or transforming a backyard into a more usable space, our focus is always the same: clear communication, clean work, and results that last.

We're proud to serve Murphy, TX and the greater DFW area with honest service, thoughtful planning, and outdoor solutions that make every property feel more complete.
